Keep track of your working hours, breaks, and overtime: Work Time Manager is a time clock, timesheet, and flextime account in one app. The core features are free and ready to use right away without signing in.

⏱ ONE-TAP TIME TRACKING
Start and stop your workday in the app, from the home screen widget, or with an app icon shortcut. The app shows your working time, your expected end of day, and your overtime balance live. Add forgotten days in the calendar or edit them at any time.

☕ AUTOMATIC BREAKS
If breaks are missing when you stop the timer, the app adds them automatically: 30 minutes after 6 hours, 45 minutes after 9 hours of work. Track your own breaks live with the break timer or add them later.

📊 OVERTIME & FLEXTIME
Your flextime account compares target and actual hours. If you like, the app warns you when you reach your own overtime or undertime limit, and reminds you of a missing start of work, end of work, or break.

🏠 HOME OFFICE & WORK LOCATIONS
Mark home office days with one tap. Add one or more work locations per day, such as office, client, or construction site, and show them on the dashboard if you like.

🗓 PUBLIC HOLIDAYS, VACATION & SICK LEAVE
Select your German federal state and the app takes public holidays into account automatically. Log vacation and sick days with Quick Entry, also for several days at once. Your remaining leave is shown in the settings.

📈 REPORTS, TREND & FORECAST (PREMIUM)
• Weekly, monthly, and yearly reports with working time and overtime
• Year tab with vacation, sick, and public holiday days and a comparison with the previous year
• Trend over the last 12 weeks or 12 months with charts for working time and overtime
• Forecast of your overtime balance at the end of the year
• Insights: analysis by weekday, heatmap by start time, and a notice when you have worked above your target for many workdays in a row
• Weekly reflection: note what went well and what was hard

📄 PDF REPORTS (PREMIUM)
Export as PDF: weekly, monthly, and yearly reports, the home office report for a month or year, and the vacation and sick leave report, e.g. for your records or your employer.

👥 WORK PROFILES (PREMIUM)
Main job and side job, or separate projects? Create additional profiles with their own target hours, workdays, and separate time accounts.

🎙 GOOGLE ASSISTANT (ANDROID)
Start, pause, and stop the timer by voice. With Premium you can also ask for your overtime balance, your remaining leave, and your weekly trend.

💻 CLOUD SYNC & WEB APP
Without signing in, your data stays on your device. Sign in with your Google account to sync it and use time tracking in your browser at work-time-manager.app.

🔒 PRIVACY & APP LOCK
• No ads, no selling of your data
• App lock with PIN, fingerprint, or face unlock
• For full-time, part-time, flextime, shift work, and home office
• Light and dark design, in English and German
