Track your work hours, breaks, and overtime effortlessly – with a modern, easy-to-use work time tracker and digital time clock. Create timesheets, manage your flextime balance, and calculate overtime automatically. Free to use and ready immediately without sign-up!

⏱ WORK TIME TRACKER & DIGITAL TIME CLOCK
Start and stop your work timer with a single tap. Work Time Manager tracks your working hours accurately, shows your projected end-of-day time, and calculates your overtime balance in real time. Forgot to clock in? Easily add or edit start times, end times, and past workdays in the calendar at any time.

☕ AUTOMATIC BREAK CALCULATION
Statutory breaks under German labor law (ArbZG) are calculated automatically when stopping the timer: 30 minutes after 6 hours of work, 45 minutes after 9 hours. You can also track custom breaks live or edit them manually at any time.

📊 OVERTIME TRACKER & FLEXTIME BALANCE
Your personal overtime account compares target hours vs. actual hours worked at a glance. Always know how much overtime you have accumulated or how many hours remain. Optional notifications warn you when exceeding custom overtime or undertime thresholds.

📄 TIMESHEETS, REPORTS & PDF EXPORT
Generate clear reports of your working hours:
• Daily reports & calendar overview (free forever)
• Weekly, monthly & yearly reports (Premium)
• PDF Export: Export and print clean PDF timesheets and work time records for your files or employer
• Productivity insights & weekly reflection to better understand your work-life balance

🗓 PUBLIC HOLIDAYS & QUICK ENTRY FOR VACATION
Select your German federal state (all 16 states supported) to automatically account for public holidays. Log vacation days, sick leave, and special entries for single days or entire weeks at once using Batch Quick Entry.

👥 MULTIPLE WORK PROFILES (MAIN JOB & SIDE JOB)
Working multiple jobs, freelance projects, or separate employers? Create independent work profiles with their own target hours, workdays, and overtime balances (Premium).

💻 CLOUD SYNC & WEB APP FOR BROWSER
Without sign-in, all data is stored safely and locally on your device. Optionally sign in with your Google account to sync your work hours across devices and access the web app on your PC at work-time-manager.app.
